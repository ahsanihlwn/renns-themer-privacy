Privacy Policy

Renns Themer

Effective July 29, 2026 · Baca dalam Bahasa Indonesia

Renns Themer is a utility app for installing custom theme files (.xth / .zth) on Transsion devices (Infinix/Tecno/itel). This page explains what data the app accesses and how it's used.

Short version: Renns Themer does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to any server. The app has no internet connectivity and includes no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Data the App Accesses

To install and manage theme files, the app needs access to:

  • The theme file you pick (.xth / .zth) — read in order to copy it into your Transsion launcher/theme app's theme folder.
  • The Transsion launcher/theme app's data folder (e.g. Android/data/com.transsion.theme/files/) — accessed through Shizuku or root access, solely to copy, read metadata from, and delete the theme files you manage inside this app.

All of this processing happens entirely on your device. No file, metadata, or other information is ever sent off your device by this app.

Data NOT Collected

  • No name, email, phone number, or other personal identity data.
  • No location data.
  • No usage history, statistics, or analytics data.
  • No advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • No user accounts or login process.

Permissions Used

The app may request the following permissions, solely to support its core theme-installing function:

  • Storage / file access — to read the theme file you pick and copy it to the appropriate destination folder.
  • Shizuku (optional, if you separately install the Shizuku app) — to run copy/delete file operations against another app's data folder, which a regular app can't reach directly under Android's scoped storage restrictions.
